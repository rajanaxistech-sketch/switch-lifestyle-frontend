Our Culinary Lineage

Behind The Hearth: 3 Acts of Craft

Great food isn't fast. It's patient, honest, and shaped by fire.

01
Act I · Patience

48h Cold Fermentation

Our dough is mixed in small batches with Italian Caputo flour, wild yeast, and sea salt, then rested in cold vaults for 48 hours. This develops incredible airy bubbles and lightness on the stomach.

Result: Digestible, crispy, fragrant crusts.
02
Act II · Heritage

San Marzano & Gippsland Farms

We combine volcanic soil San Marzano tomatoes directly from Campania with the freshest free-range produce, grass-fed beef, and dairy from our local Victorian farmers.

Result: Uncompromised ingredient integrity.
03
Act III · The Fire

400°C Blistering Hearth

Each pizza enters our blistering stone deck for exactly 90 seconds. The fierce radiant heat cooks the crust from below while caramelising the mozzarella and sugo on top.

Result: The authentic char and smoke finish.
